# explanation

Explanation pages give background. They are for a reader who wants to understand agentrc rather than operate it: why it is shaped the way it is, what it trusts, what tradeoffs it makes and where its boundaries lie. They can be read away from the keyboard, they take a position, and they are the one place in the documentation where discussing alternatives is welcome.

## pages

- [security model](security-model.md): what agentrc protects against, how the detectors and hooks do it, what it trusts in a source root and what it leaves to the runtimes.
